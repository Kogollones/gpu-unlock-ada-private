# Credits and source provenance

The transaction, compatibility layer, PCI containment, boot I/O and host tests
reuse the GPL-2.0-only Latence.app MSI AD104 port in `../ad104-msi`. Original
authors, notices and licences remain there. The X870E ownership adapter derives
its snapshot/delta and explicit-disconnect approach from that adapter. Its
hardware paths and four driver-code identities are pinned to the owner's
October 30, 2026 diagnostic capture. Proprietary capture bytes remain private.

Firmware assets are generated by the repository's original hash-pinned
610.43.03 workflow. Retain `../../firmware/NOTICE.md`, the generated
`NVIDIA-LICENSE`, and the upstream notices in `../ad104-msi`. The owner's private
fork now archives the tested EFI and firmware assets with those notices at
`../../firmware/private-windows/rtx4080s-msi-20261030/`. It does not contain ROM
dumps, captured driver code, Windows boot-manager bytes or private signing keys.
